Goal: Obtain an actionable, fair support-ticket scheduling policy for a team of two agents.

Scenario:
Urgent tickets may arrive continuously. Ordinary tickets must still receive
service, and the policy must remain usable without special scheduling software.

Acceptance criteria:
1. Define how urgent and ordinary tickets are classified.
2. Give a concrete mechanism preventing ordinary-ticket starvation.
3. Explain a minimum service opportunity for ordinary work and its assumptions.
4. Describe what the operator measures and adjusts.
5. State at least one tradeoff honestly; avoid unconditional guarantees that
   assume unlimited capacity or predictable ticket duration.

Task scope: discussion only. Do not ask Codex to use tools, run commands, read
files or change files. Include the scenario and the discussion-only restriction
in every one-shot prompt you submit. Each iteration starts a fresh chat.

Judging: pass means all criteria are satisfied with no material contradiction;
partial means useful but at least one criterion is missing or weak; fail means
incorrect or unusable. An optional 0–5 score should explain its basis in evidence.
